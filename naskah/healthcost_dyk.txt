What if a doctor visit cost a fortune? Let's compare health spending per person, adjusted for prices. Japan: about five thousand two hundred dollars a year. The UK: about five thousand five hundred, with the NHS free when you need it. Australia: about six thousand four hundred, with Medicare for everyone. Germany: about eight thousand. Switzerland: about eight thousand too, where everyone must buy health insurance! But here's the twist. America: about twelve thousand six hundred dollars per person, the highest in the world, yet Americans don't live longer than people in these countries! Should health care be free?
