Which country has the most time zones? Let's count. China: just one time zone for the whole country, so in the far west the sun can rise after ten AM! India: one time zone too, and it's half an hour off from most of the world. Australia: three main time zones, plus a few unusual ones. America: six time zones across its fifty states, from Hawaii to the East Coast. Russia: eleven time zones, from Europe all the way to the Pacific! Think that's the most? Here's the twist.
