What if you drank tea all day long? Let's compare tea per person. America: about a quarter kilo a year, and most of it is iced tea! Australia: about three quarters of a kilo. Japan: about one kilo, mostly green tea, and it even has a whole tea ceremony. The UK: almost two kilos, about one hundred million cups every single day! Ireland: about two point two kilos, even more than the UK! But here's the twist. Turkey: over three kilos per person, the biggest tea drinkers on Earth, served in little tulip shaped glasses!
