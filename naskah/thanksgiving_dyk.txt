What if Thanksgiving happened on a totally different day? Let's check around the world. Canada: the second Monday of October, more than a month earlier! Germany: a harvest festival called Erntedankfest, in early October. South Korea: Chuseok, a three day harvest holiday when families travel home. Japan: Labor Thanksgiving Day, every November twenty third, to thank workers. Australia: on tiny Norfolk Island, people celebrate an American style Thanksgiving, thanks to visiting whalers!
