Is December eleventh written twelve eleven, or eleven twelve? It depends where you live! Australia: day first, then the month, so it's eleven twelve. The UK: day first too. Japan: year first, then month, then day, which makes dates easy to sort. China: year first as well, the same as the international standard. Canada: you can find all three formats, sometimes on the same street! But here's the twist. America: month first, then day, and it's one of the only countries in the world that does it, which is why nine eleven means September! How do you write the date?
