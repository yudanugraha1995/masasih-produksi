Are French fries actually French? Let's ask around. America: French fries, the side dish with almost every burger. The UK: chips, and potato chips are called crisps, so fish and chips means fries! Australia: hot chips, to tell them apart from the ones in a bag. Canada: poutine, fries with gravy and squeaky cheese curds, from Quebec. The Netherlands: patat, often with a big scoop of mayo, or with peanut sauce and onions, called war fries! But here's the twist. Belgium: Belgians say fries were invented there, not in France, and they even have a fries museum in Bruges! What do you call them? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
