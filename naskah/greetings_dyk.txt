What if you said hello with your nose? Let's travel. America: a firm handshake, and a weak one can look rude! Australia: a handshake and a friendly g'day, mate! Japan: a bow, and the deeper the bow, the more respect you show. France: la bise, a kiss on each cheek, and some regions do three or even four! India: namaste, palms pressed together with a small bow, no touching needed. But here's the twist. New Zealand: the Māori hongi, pressing noses and foreheads together to share the breath of life!
