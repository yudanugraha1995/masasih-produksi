What if every store closed on Sunday? Let's compare. America: most stores open seven days a week, some all night. Australia: open on Sundays too, though some stores open later. The UK: big stores can only open for six hours, usually ten to four. Switzerland: most shops stay closed, except at train stations and airports. Germany: nearly all shops are closed by law, so everyone rushes to the supermarket on Saturday! But here's the twist. Poland: shopping is banned on most Sundays, only a few Sundays a year are allowed, mostly before Christmas and Easter! Would you survive a Sunday with no shopping?
