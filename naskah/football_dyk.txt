Is it soccer or football? The world can't agree. America: soccer, because football means the NFL, with helmets and touchdowns! Australia: soccer too, since footy usually means Aussie rules or rugby league. The UK: football, and the word soccer actually started there, as slang for association football! Brazil: futebol, and they've won five World Cups. Spain: fútbol, home of Real Madrid and Barcelona, two of the biggest clubs on Earth. But here's the twist. Italy: they call it calcio, which means kick, named after an old Florence game with almost no rules! What do you call it? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
