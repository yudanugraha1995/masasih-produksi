What If Christmas Dinner Was KFC? 🍗
What if Christmas dinner was totally different where you live?

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: Ham or turkey (classic roast dinner)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: Seafood + bbq (Christmas is in summer!)
3. 🇬🇧 UK: Roast turkey (+ Christmas pudding)
4. 🇲🇽 Mexico: Tamales (on Christmas Eve)
5. 🇵🇭 Philippines: Lechon (whole roast pig)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇯🇵 Japan: KFC! (booked weeks ahead)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if Christmas dinner was totally different where you live? Let's travel the world. America: ham or a big roast turkey. Australia: it's summer there, so seafood and a barbecue on the beach! The UK: roast turkey and Christmas pudding. Mexico: tamales on Christmas Eve. The Philippines: lechon, a whole roast pig. But here's the twist. Japan: millions of families eat KFC fried chicken for Christmas, and they book it weeks ahead! What's on your Christmas table? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
