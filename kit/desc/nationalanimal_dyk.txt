🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇦🇺 Australia: Kangaroo (on the coat of arms)
2. 🇨🇦 Canada: Beaver (on the 5 cent coin!)
3. 🇳🇿 New Zealand: Kiwi (a bird that can't fly)
4. 🇮🇳 India: Bengal Tiger
5. 🇬🇧 UK: Unicorn (Scotland!)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: BALD EAGLE (official only since Dec 2024!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your national animal was a unicorn? Let's check. Australia: the kangaroo, right on the coat of arms next to the emu. Canada: the beaver, which is even on the five cent coin! New Zealand: the kiwi, a bird that can't fly, and New Zealanders call themselves Kiwis too. India: the Bengal tiger, with about three quarters of the world's wild tigers. The UK: Scotland's national animal is the unicorn! But here's the twist. America: the bald eagle has been on the Great Seal since seventeen eighty two, but it only officially became the national bird in December twenty twenty four! What's your national animal?
