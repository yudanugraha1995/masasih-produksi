🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: 1% (bike to work)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: ~1% (bike to work)
3. 🇩🇪 Germany: ~1 IN 10 (trips by bike)
4. 🇯🇵 Japan: Mamachari (mom bikes!)
5. 🇩🇰 Denmark: ~Half (of Copenhagen commuters)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇳🇱 Netherlands: 23M BIKES (more bikes than people!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if there were more bikes than people? Let's ride. America: less than one percent of workers bike to work, most people drive. Australia: about one percent too, and wearing a helmet is the law. Germany: around one in ten trips by bike, with bike lanes in most cities. Japan: lots of mamachari, mom bikes with seats for kids and baskets for groceries! Denmark: in Copenhagen, almost half of commuters ride a bike, even in the snow! But here's the twist. The Netherlands: about twenty three million bikes, more bikes than people, and Utrecht has a parking garage for over twelve thousand bikes! Bike, car or bus? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
