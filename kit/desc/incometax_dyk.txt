🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: 37% (federal top rate)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: 45% (top rate)
3. 🇬🇧 UK: 45% (top rate)
4. 🇯🇵 Japan: 45% (+ local tax)
5. 🇩🇰 Denmark: ~56% (top rate)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇦🇪 UAE: 0% (personal income tax!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if you paid zero income tax? Let's compare the top rates. America: thirty seven percent federal, plus state tax in most states. Australia: forty five percent, on income over one hundred ninety thousand dollars. The UK: forty five percent too, on income over about one hundred twenty five thousand pounds. Japan: forty five percent, plus a ten percent local tax. Denmark: around fifty six percent, but healthcare and college are free! But here's the twist. The UAE: zero percent personal income tax, so your whole salary goes home with you! Would you move for zero tax? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
