🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: 6 PM
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: 6:30 PM
3. 🇬🇧 UK: 7 PM
4. 🇩🇪 Germany: Abendbrot (cold bread dinner, 6-7 PM)
5. 🇮🇹 Italy: 8:30 PM

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇪🇸 Spain: 10 PM (or even later!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if dinner started at ten at night? Let's check the clock. America: around six PM, sometimes even earlier with kids, and lots of restaurants offer early bird specials! Australia: around six thirty, and they often call it tea! The UK: around seven, and in the north it's also called tea. Germany: Abendbrot, a cold bread dinner around six or seven, since lunch is the big meal. Italy: around eight thirty, after an aperitivo with friends. But here's the twist. Spain: dinner often starts at ten PM, or even later, partly because Spain uses a time zone that's an hour ahead of the sun! When do you eat dinner? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
