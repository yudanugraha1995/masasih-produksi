Which Country Has the Most McDonald's? 🍔
What if there was a McDonald's on every corner?

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇦🇺 Australia: ~1,000 ("Maccas"!)
2. 🇩🇪 Germany: ~1,400 (restaurants)
3. 🇬🇧 UK: ~1,500 (restaurants)
4. 🇯🇵 Japan: ~3,000 (restaurants)
5. 🇨🇳 China: 7,000+ (and growing fast)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: ~13,500 (1 in 8 workers worked there!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if there was a McDonald's on every corner? Let's count the restaurants. Australia: about one thousand, where people call it Maccas! Germany: about one thousand four hundred, and some even sell beer! The UK: about one thousand five hundred, since the first one opened in nineteen seventy four. Japan: about three thousand, and in Osaka people call it Makudo! China: over seven thousand, opening hundreds more every year, after its first one in nineteen ninety! But here's the twist. America: about thirteen thousand five hundred McDonald's, and roughly one in eight American workers has worked at one! Big Mac or McNuggets? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
