🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
Where is the tallest building on Earth? Let's go up. The UK: the Shard in London, three hundred ten meters. Australia: Q one on the Gold Coast, three hundred twenty two meters. America: One World Trade Center, five hundred forty one meters, that's one thousand seven hundred seventy six feet, for the year of independence! China: Shanghai Tower, six hundred thirty two meters. Malaysia: Merdeka one eighteen, six hundred seventy nine meters! Think that's tall? Here's the twist. Dubai: the Burj Khalifa, eight hundred twenty eight meters, and you can see its tip from almost a hundred kilometers away! What's the tallest building near you?
