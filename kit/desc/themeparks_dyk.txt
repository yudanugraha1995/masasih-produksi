🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if you lived next to the world's biggest theme park? Let's look. Denmark: Bakken, from fifteen eighty three, is the oldest amusement park still open! Japan: Tokyo Disneyland, opened in nineteen eighty three, was the first Disney park outside America. France: Disneyland Paris is the most visited tourist attraction in Europe. The UAE: Formula Rossa at Ferrari World hits two hundred forty kilometers an hour, one of the fastest roller coasters ever. Australia: the Gold Coast has five big theme parks close together! But here's the twist. America: Disney's Magic Kingdom in Florida is the most visited theme park on Earth, with about seventeen million guests a year! Roller coaster or water slide?
