Why is election day on a Tuesday?

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇬🇧 UK: Thursday (by tradition)
2. 🇨🇦 Canada: Monday (in October)
3. 🇩🇪 Germany: Sunday (day off for most)
4. 🇦🇺 Australia: Saturday (compulsory + democracy sausage!)
5. 🇮🇳 India: 6 WEEKS (~1 billion voters)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: TUESDAY (a law from 1845!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
Why is election day on a Tuesday? Let's compare election days. The UK: always on a Thursday, by tradition. Canada: usually a Monday in October. Germany: always a Sunday, so most people are off work. Australia: always a Saturday, voting is compulsory, and you can grab a democracy sausage at the polling station! India: elections take about six weeks, with almost a billion voters. But here's the twist. America: votes on the Tuesday after the first Monday in November, because of a law from eighteen forty five, so farmers could travel by horse and buggy without missing church or market day!
