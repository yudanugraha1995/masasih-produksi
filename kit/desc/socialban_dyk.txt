What If Kids Were BANNED From Social Media? 📵
Did you know Australia was the FIRST country in the world to ban social media for kids under 16? 📵😮 Since December 2025 it even covers YouTube. Indonesia and Malaysia followed with under-16 rules in 2026, Greece plans an under-15 ban from 2027, the UK is planning one, and the USA has no national ban.

💬 Should your country ban social media for kids? Tell us in the comments 👇

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: No ban (no national law)
2. 🇬🇧 UK: Planned (could start in 2027)
3. 🇬🇷 Greece: Under 15 (banned from 2027)
4. 🇲🇾 Malaysia: Under 16 (banned since June 2026)
5. 🇮🇩 Indonesia: Under 16 (banned since March 2026)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇦🇺 Australia: WORLD FIRST (under 16, even YouTube!)
