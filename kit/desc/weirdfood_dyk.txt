🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇯🇵 Japan: Natto (sticky soybeans)
2. 🇸🇪 Sweden: Surströmming (smelly herring!)
3. 🇮🇸 Iceland: Hákarl (fermented shark)
4. 🇰🇷 South Korea: Sannakji (wriggling octopus)
5. 🇦🇺 Australia: Grubs (witchetty grubs)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: BUTTER (deep fried, on a stick!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your dinner was a famous weird food? Let's look. Japan: natto, sticky fermented soybeans with stretchy strings! Sweden: surströmming, fermented herring so smelly that many people open the can outdoors. Iceland: hákarl, shark meat left to ferment and dry for months. South Korea: sannakji, octopus served so fresh it's still wriggling. Australia: witchetty grubs, fat white larvae that taste a bit like nutty egg. But here's the twist. America: at state fairs you can buy deep fried butter on a stick, and even deep fried soda! Which one would you try? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
