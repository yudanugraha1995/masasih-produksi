Which Country Has Sent the Most People to Space? 🚀
What if your country sent you to space?

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇷🇺 Russia: 1961 (first human in space)
2. 🇨🇳 China: 2003 (3rd to launch people)
3. 🇯🇵 Japan: 1990 (first journalist in space)
4. 🇨🇦 Canada: Hadfield (sang in space!)
5. 🇦🇺 Australia: Andy Thomas (had to become American)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: 12 (men on the Moon!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your country sent you to space? Let's count. Russia: Yuri Gagarin became the first human in space in nineteen sixty one, and Valentina Tereshkova the first woman. China: in twenty oh three it became the third country to launch people on its own rockets. Japan: sent the first journalist to space, back in nineteen ninety. Canada: astronaut Chris Hadfield sang a David Bowie song on the space station, and millions watched! Australia: its first astronaut, Andy Thomas, had to become an American citizen to fly with NASA. But here's the twist. America: has sent over three hundred fifty people to space, and it's still the only country that has walked on the Moon, twelve men in all! Would you go to space?
