What If You Had to Tip 20% on Every Meal? 💸
Did you know leaving a tip in Japan can actually seem RUDE? 🙅 In the USA and Canada 15-20% is expected, the UK adds about 10% to the bill, and in Australia nobody expects a tip at all!

💬 Do people tip in your country? How much? Tell us in the comments 👇

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: 15-20% (expected)
2. 🇨🇦 Canada: 15-20% (about the same)
3. 🇬🇧 UK: ~10% (often added to the bill)
4. 🇦🇺 Australia: Not expected
5. 🇨🇳 China: Not customary (traditionally)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇯🇵 Japan: NO TIPS! (it can seem rude)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if you had to tip twenty percent on every meal? America: fifteen to twenty percent is expected. Canada: about the same. The UK: around ten percent, often added to the bill. Australia: tipping is not expected at all. China: traditionally not customary. Plot twist: in Japan, leaving a tip can actually seem rude! Comment your country!
