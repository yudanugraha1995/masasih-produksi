🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your road trip broke a world record? Let's look. China: has the world's biggest expressway network, about one hundred eighty thousand kilometers. Norway: the Lærdal Tunnel runs over twenty four kilometers through a mountain, the longest road tunnel on Earth. Turkey: has the world's longest suspension bridge span, over two kilometers. Japan: some roads play music when you drive over them at the right speed! Australia: the Eyre Highway has a dead straight stretch of about one hundred forty six kilometers, with no turns at all! But here's the twist. America: has the largest road network on Earth, over six and a half million kilometers, enough to reach the Moon and back about eight times! Road trip or plane?
