🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your country was full of billionaires? Let's count, using the Forbes twenty twenty five list. Australia: about fifty billionaires, led by mining tycoon Gina Rinehart. The UK: about fifty five, many of them in London. Germany: about one hundred seventy, many from old family businesses. India: about two hundred, with Mumbai as a billionaire hub. China: about four hundred fifty, the second most! But here's the twist. America: about nine hundred billionaires, roughly one in every three billionaires on Earth, and the richest person alive lives there too! Would you want to be a billionaire?
