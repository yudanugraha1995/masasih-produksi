What If Your Country Drove on the LEFT? 🚗
Did you know Sweden switched from driving on the left to the right in ONE morning? 🤯 On 3 September 1967 the whole country changed sides at 5 a.m.! The UK, Australia, Japan and Indonesia still drive on the left, while the USA drives on the right.
