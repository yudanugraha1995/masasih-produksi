When do kids open their Christmas presents?

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: Dec 25 (Christmas morning)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: Dec 25 (then off to the beach!)
3. 🇩🇪 Germany: Dec 24 (Christmas Eve)
4. 🇳🇱 Netherlands: Dec 5 (Sinterklaas evening)
5. 🇷🇺 Russia: Jan 1 (from Grandfather Frost)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇪🇸 Spain: JAN 6 (Three Kings Day!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
When do kids open their Christmas presents? It depends where you live! America: December twenty fifth, on Christmas morning. Australia: December twenty fifth too, and then it's off to the beach! Germany: Christmas Eve, December twenty fourth. The Netherlands: December fifth, when Sinterklaas visits. Russia: New Year's Day, from Grandfather Frost. But here's the twist. Spain: many kids wait until January sixth, when the Three Kings bring the gifts! When do you open yours? Comment below, and subscribe!
