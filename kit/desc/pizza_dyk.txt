🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
Is Hawaiian pizza actually from Hawaii? Let's look at pizza around the world. Australia: egg on pizza is a thing, and so are prawns! Japan: mayo, corn and even squid, sometimes all on one pizza! Sweden: banana and curry pizza, a classic at Swedish pizzerias! Italy: the Margherita, said to be named after a queen. Canada: Hawaiian pizza was invented in Ontario in nineteen sixty two, by a Greek immigrant! But here's the twist. America: eats about three billion pizzas a year, that's around three hundred fifty slices every second, and pepperoni is the top topping! Pineapple on pizza, yes or no?
