🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: 10 STATES (have a bottle deposit)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: 10 CENTS (per bottle)
3. 🇨🇦 Canada: Deposits (in most provinces)
4. 🇳🇱 Netherlands: Deposits (bottles + cans)
5. 🇩🇪 Germany: 25 CENTS (per plastic bottle!)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇳🇴 Norway: 90%+ (of bottles come back!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if every empty bottle was worth money? Let's compare. America: only ten states pay a bottle deposit, like Michigan, with ten cents per can. Australia: ten cents per bottle in most states, and kids collect them for pocket money. Canada: deposits in most provinces, usually on drinks and beer. The Netherlands: deposits on bottles and cans, since twenty twenty three for cans. Germany: twenty five cents back for each plastic bottle, at machines in every supermarket! But here's the twist. Norway: over ninety percent of bottles come back, and some are recycled into new bottles again and again! Do you return your bottles? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
