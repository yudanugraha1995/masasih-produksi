🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇦🇺 Australia: None (no high-speed rail)
2. 🇺🇸 USA: 160 MPH (new Acela trains)
3. 🇩🇪 Germany: 300 km/h (ICE trains)
4. 🇫🇷 France: 320 km/h (TGV)
5. 🇯🇵 Japan: 320 km/h (Shinkansen)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇨🇳 China: 45,000+ km (more than the rest of the world!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if America had bullet trains everywhere? Let's compare. Australia: no high speed rail at all, so most people fly between big cities. America: the new Acela reaches one hundred sixty miles per hour, but only on short stretches. Germany: ICE trains at three hundred kilometers per hour. France: TGV trains at three hundred twenty, and one test train hit five hundred seventy four! Japan: the Shinkansen at three hundred twenty too, famous for being on time to the second. But here's the twist. China: over forty five thousand kilometers of high speed rail, more than the rest of the world combined! Would you ride a bullet train? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
