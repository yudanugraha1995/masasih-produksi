More Sheep Than People? 🐑
What if there were more animals than people where you live?

💬 What animal wins where you live? Tell us in the comments!

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: 87M COWS (vs 340M people)
2. 🇮🇳 India: 190M+ CATTLE (vs 1.4B people)
3. 🇬🇧 UK: 31M SHEEP (vs 69M people)
4. 🇮🇪 Ireland: 7M CATTLE (vs 5.3M people!)
5. 🇳🇿 New Zealand: 4+ SHEEP (per person!)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇦🇺 Australia: KANGAROOS (outnumber people!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if there were more animals than people where you live? America: about eighty seven million cows, but way more people. India: over one hundred ninety million cattle, but about one point four billion people. The UK: around thirty one million sheep, about one for every two people. Ireland: about seven million cattle, more cows than people! New Zealand: more than four sheep for every person, although that number used to be over twenty! Think that's wild? Here's the twist. Australia: there are more kangaroos than people, with tens of millions hopping around a country of twenty seven million! What animal wins where you live? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
