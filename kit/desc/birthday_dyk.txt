🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: Cake + Candles (and the birthday song)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: Fairy Bread (butter + sprinkles)
3. 🇲🇽 Mexico: La Mordida (face in the cake!)
4. 🇨🇦 Canada: Nose Greasing (butter for good luck!)
5. 🇩🇰 Denmark: Flags (in the window)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇰🇷 South Korea: AGE 1 AT BIRTH (Korean age, used until 2023)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your birthday came with a face full of cake? Let's see. America: cake, candles and the birthday song, which was under copyright until twenty sixteen! Australia: fairy bread, white bread with butter and sprinkles! Mexico: la mordida, someone pushes your face into the cake! Canada: in Atlantic Canada, kids get their nose greased with butter for luck! Denmark: flags hang in the window to tell everyone it's your birthday. But here's the twist. South Korea: until twenty twenty three, babies turned one on the day they were born, and everyone got a year older on New Year's Day! How do you celebrate? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
