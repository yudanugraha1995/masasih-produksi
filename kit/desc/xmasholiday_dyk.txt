What If Christmas Wasn't a Day Off? 📅
What if Christmas was just a normal work day?

💬 Do you get Christmas off? Tell us in the comments!

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: Day Off (federal holiday)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: 2 DAYS OFF (Christmas + Boxing Day)
3. 🇬🇧 UK: 2 DAYS OFF (Christmas + Boxing Day)
4. 🇰🇷 South Korea: Day Off (public holiday)
5. 🇨🇳 China: Work Day (not a public holiday)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇯🇵 Japan: WORK DAY (yet it's full of lights!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if Christmas was just a normal work day? Let's check. America: Christmas Day is a federal holiday, and most stores close. Australia: two days off, Christmas and Boxing Day, right in the middle of summer! The UK: two days off too, Christmas and Boxing Day, famous for big sales. South Korea: Christmas is a public holiday, and it's a popular day for couples. China: Christmas is a normal work day, but malls still put up giant trees. But here's the twist. Japan: Christmas isn't a day off either, yet cities are covered in lights and families line up for fried chicken! Do you get Christmas off? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
