Which Country Has the BUSIEST Airport? ✈️
Did you know the busiest airport on Earth isn't Dubai or London? ✈️🤯 In 2025 Istanbul and Heathrow each handled about 84 million passengers, Shanghai 85 million, Tokyo Haneda 92 million and Dubai 95 million… but Atlanta, USA beat them all with more than 106 million!

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇹🇷 Turkey: 84m (Istanbul, passengers in 2025)
2. 🇬🇧 UK: 84m (London Heathrow)
3. 🇨🇳 China: 85m (Shanghai Pudong)
4. 🇯🇵 Japan: 92m (Tokyo Haneda)
5. 🇦🇪 UAE: 95m (Dubai, #1 for international)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 Atlanta, USA: 106M (passengers, #1 on Earth!)
