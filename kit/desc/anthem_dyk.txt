🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: 4 VERSES (only the 1st is sung)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: "One And Free" (changed in 2021)
3. 🇬🇧 UK: God Save The King (since 2022)
4. 🇯🇵 Japan: Kimigayo (one of the shortest)
5. 🇪🇸 Spain: No Words! (Marcha Real)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇬🇷 Greece: 158 VERSES (longest anthem on Earth!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your national anthem had a hundred and fifty eight verses? Let's listen. America: the Star Spangled Banner has four verses, and usually only the first is sung. Australia: changed one line in twenty twenty one, from young and free, to one and free. The UK: God Save the King, changed from Queen in twenty twenty two. Japan: Kimigayo, one of the shortest anthems in the world, with just thirty two syllables. Spain: the Marcha Real has no official words, so players just hum! But here's the twist. Greece: the Hymn to Liberty has one hundred fifty eight verses, the longest anthem on Earth, but only two are usually sung! Do you know your anthem by heart? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
