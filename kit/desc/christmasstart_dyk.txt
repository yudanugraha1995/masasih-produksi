🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: After Thanksgiving (tree up + Black Friday)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: Early December (carols in summer!)
3. 🇬🇧 UK: Mid November (London lights switch on)
4. 🇩🇪 Germany: Late November (Christmas markets open)
5. 🇳🇱 Netherlands: AFTER DEC 5 (Sinterklaas comes first!)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇵🇭 Philippines: SEPTEMBER 1 ("ber months" until January!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
When does Christmas season really start? Mariah Carey says it's time, but every country has its own clock. America: right after Thanksgiving, when the tree goes up and Black Friday begins. Australia: early December, with carols and barbecues in the summer heat. The UK: mid November, when the famous Christmas lights switch on in London. Germany: late November, when the Christmas markets open for Advent. The Netherlands: only after Sinterklaas leaves on December fifth! But here's the twist. The Philippines: Christmas starts on September first, in the ber months, and lasts until January, the longest Christmas season on Earth! When do you put up your tree?
