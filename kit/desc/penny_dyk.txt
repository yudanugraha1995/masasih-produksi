🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇳🇿 New Zealand: 1990 (1¢ and 2¢ coins gone)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: 1992 (1¢ and 2¢ coins gone)
3. 🇫🇮 Finland: Rounding (no 1¢ or 2¢ euro coins)
4. 🇳🇱 Netherlands: Rounding (since 2004)
5. 🇨🇦 Canada: 2013 (penny gone)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: 2025 (last penny struck!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if pennies just disappeared? It's already happening! New Zealand: one and two cent coins, gone in nineteen ninety. Australia: gone in nineteen ninety two, and prices are rounded to five cents. Finland: no one or two cent euro coins in everyday use, so prices are rounded instead. The Netherlands: rounding too, since two thousand four. Canada: said goodbye to the penny in twenty thirteen, because each one cost more than a cent to make. But here's the twist. America: the US Mint made its very last penny in twenty twenty five, after more than two hundred thirty years! Do you still use pennies? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
