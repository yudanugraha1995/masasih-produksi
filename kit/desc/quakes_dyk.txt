🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇯🇵 Japan: ~20% (of big quakes)
2. 🇨🇱 Chile: 9.5 (strongest ever)
3. 🇳🇿 New Zealand: ~20,000 (quakes a year)
4. 🇮🇩 Indonesia: 2004 (tsunami)
5. 🇦🇺 Australia: 7 CM (moving north a year!)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: ~10,000 (quakes a year in SoCal!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if the ground shook under your feet every day? Let's look. Japan: gets about a fifth of the world's biggest earthquakes. Chile: had the strongest earthquake ever recorded, magnitude nine point five, in nineteen sixty. New Zealand: records around twenty thousand earthquakes every year. Indonesia: sits on the Ring of Fire, and the twenty oh four quake sent a tsunami across the Indian Ocean. Australia: is moving north about seven centimeters a year, so its maps had to be shifted by almost two meters! But here's the twist. America: Alaska had the second strongest quake ever recorded, and Southern California alone gets about ten thousand earthquakes a year! Have you ever felt an earthquake?
