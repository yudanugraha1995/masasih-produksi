🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if the tooth fairy was actually a mouse? Let's check. America: the tooth fairy leaves money under your pillow, about six dollars a tooth on average! Australia: the tooth fairy too. The UK: the tooth fairy as well, usually leaving a shiny coin. Mexico: Ratón Pérez, a little mouse, takes your tooth! France: la petite souris, the little mouse, hides a coin under the pillow. But here's the twist. Japan: kids throw their lower teeth up onto the roof, and upper teeth down under the house, so new teeth grow in straight! Who visited you, the fairy or the mouse?
