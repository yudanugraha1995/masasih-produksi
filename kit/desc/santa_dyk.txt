What If Santa Was a Witch? 🧹
Who brings the Christmas presents where you live?

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: Santa Claus (sleigh + reindeer)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: Santa in shorts (6 white kangaroos in the song!)
3. 🇬🇧 UK: Father Christmas (same idea, older name)
4. 🇫🇷 France: Père Noël (Father Christmas)
5. 🇷🇺 Russia: Ded Moroz (Grandfather Frost)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇮🇹 Italy: LA BEFANA (a kind witch on a broom!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
Who brings the Christmas presents where you live? America: Santa Claus, with his sleigh and reindeer. Australia: Santa in shorts, and in a famous song, his sleigh is pulled by six white kangaroos! The UK: Father Christmas. France: Père Noël. Russia: Ded Moroz, Grandfather Frost, with the Snow Maiden. But here's the twist.
