🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if you knew who invented the stuff you use every day? Let's look. Australia: helped invent WiFi, Google Maps and the black box flight recorder! Japan: instant noodles, in nineteen fifty eight, and karaoke too. Sweden: the three point seat belt, by Volvo, which gave the design away for free to save lives. Canada: insulin, first used to treat diabetes in nineteen twenty two. The UK: the World Wide Web, by Tim Berners Lee in nineteen eighty nine. But here's the twist. America: the airplane, the first computer network that became the internet, and the smartphone as we know it, all started there! Which invention can't you live without?
