🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your country's flag had a secret story? Let's look. Denmark: has one of the oldest national flags still in use, from the thirteen hundreds. Switzerland: one of only two countries with a square flag, along with Vatican City. Japan: its red sun flag only became the official national flag in nineteen ninety nine! Canada: the maple leaf flag only appeared in nineteen sixty five. Australia: the Aboriginal flag, designed in nineteen seventy one, is also an official flag of the country. But here's the twist. America: today's fifty star flag was designed by a high school student for a class project, and his teacher gave it a B minus! What grade would you give your flag?
