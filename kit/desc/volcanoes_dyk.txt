Which Country Has the Most Volcanoes? 🌋
What if a volcano was right next to your house?

💬 Would you live near a volcano? Tell us in the comments!

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇮🇸 Iceland: ~30 (volcanic systems)
2. 🇮🇹 Italy: 2,000 (years of eruptions!)
3. 🇯🇵 Japan: 111 (active volcanoes)
4. 🇮🇩 Indonesia: 127 (active volcanoes)
5. 🇦🇺 Australia: Big Ben (on Heard Island)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: 161 (potentially active!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if a volcano was right next to your house? Let's look. Iceland: about thirty active volcanic systems, on an island of just four hundred thousand people! Italy: Stromboli has been erupting almost nonstop for about two thousand years. Japan: about one hundred eleven active volcanoes, roughly a tenth of the world's total. Indonesia: about one hundred twenty seven active volcanoes, and an eruption in eighteen fifteen caused a year without a summer! Australia: no active volcanoes on the mainland, but Big Ben, on its remote Heard Island, still erupts. But here's the twist. America: about one hundred sixty one potentially active volcanoes, mostly in Alaska, plus the Yellowstone supervolcano! Would you live near a volcano? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
