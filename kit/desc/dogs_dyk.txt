Which Country Has the Most Dogs? 🐶
What if there were more dogs than kids in your country?

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇦🇺 Australia: ~6M (in half of all homes)
2. 🇯🇵 Japan: ~7M (pets > kids under 15!)
3. 🇬🇧 UK: ~13M (dogs)
4. 🇨🇳 China: 50M+ (mostly in cities)
5. 🇧🇷 Brazil: ~68M (dogs)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: ~90M ($150B+ a year on pets!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if there were more dogs than kids in your country? Let's count pet dogs. Australia: about six million, in about half of all homes! Japan: about seven million, and pets now outnumber children under fifteen! The UK: about thirteen million, and dogs are welcome in lots of pubs! China: over fifty million, mostly in cities, and pet ownership there is booming. Brazil: about sixty eight million, one of the most dog loving countries on Earth! But here's the twist. America: about ninety million dogs, the most on Earth, and Americans spend over one hundred fifty billion dollars a year on their pets! Dog person or cat person?
