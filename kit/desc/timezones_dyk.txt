Which country has the most time zones?

💬 How many does your country have? Tell us in the comments!

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇨🇳 China: 1 (for the whole country!)
2. 🇮🇳 India: 1 (UTC +5:30)
3. 🇦🇺 Australia: 3 (main time zones)
4. 🇺🇸 USA: 6 (across 50 states)
5. 🇷🇺 Russia: 11 (time zones)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇫🇷 France: 12 (islands + territories)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
Which country has the most time zones? Let's count. China: just one time zone for the whole country, so in the far west the sun can rise after ten AM! India: one time zone too, and it's half an hour off from most of the world. Australia: three main time zones, plus a few unusual ones. America: six time zones across its fifty states, from Hawaii to the East Coast. Russia: eleven time zones, from Europe all the way to the Pacific! Think that's the most? Here's the twist. France: twelve time zones, thanks to its islands and territories all over the world! How many does your country have? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
