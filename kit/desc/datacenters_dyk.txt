🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇦🇺 Australia: ~300 (data centers)
2. 🇨🇦 Canada: ~330 (data centers)
3. 🇬🇧 UK: ~510 (data centers)
4. 🇩🇪 Germany: ~520 (data centers)
5. 🇮🇪 Ireland: 22% (of all electricity!)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: 5,000+ (more than the next 10 combined!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if AI data centers took over your country? Let's count. Australia: about three hundred data centers, mostly around Sydney and Melbourne. Canada: about three hundred thirty, helped by cheap hydro power. The UK: about five hundred, many around London. Germany: about five hundred twenty, with Frankfurt as a huge hub. Ireland: only about eighty, but they use twenty two percent of the country's electricity! But here's the twist. America: over five thousand data centers, more than the next ten countries combined, and northern Virginia alone is the biggest data center market on Earth! Should AI get its own power plants?
