What if a tornado could hit your town?

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇬🇧 UK: ~30 (a year, tiny country!)
2. 🇨🇦 Canada: ~60 (a year, 2nd most)
3. 🇯🇵 Japan: ~25 (a year, waterspouts)
4. 🇧🇩 Bangladesh: 1989 (deadliest tornado ever)
5. 🇦🇺 Australia: Dozens (+ willy willies)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: 1,200+ (tornadoes a year!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if a tornado could hit your town? Let's count tornadoes. The UK: about thirty a year, and for its size it's one of the most tornado packed countries on Earth! Canada: around sixty confirmed a year, the second most in the world. Japan: about twenty five a year, many starting as waterspouts over the sea. Bangladesh: hit by the deadliest tornado ever, in nineteen eighty nine, killing about thirteen hundred people. Australia: dozens a year, plus giant dust devils called willy willies! But here's the twist. America: over twelve hundred tornadoes a year, about three quarters of all the tornadoes reported on Earth, mostly in Tornado Alley! Would you ever chase a tornado?
