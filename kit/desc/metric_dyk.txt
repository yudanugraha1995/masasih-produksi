What If America Used Kilometers? 📏
What if everyone used kilometers?

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇨🇦 Canada: Km + Feet (height still in feet!)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: Metric (since the 1970s)
3. 🇯🇵 Japan: Metric (+ tatami mats)
4. 🇮🇳 India: Metric (since the late 1950s)
5. 🇬🇧 UK: Miles + Pints (metric in shops)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: MILES (1 of only 3 countries!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if everyone used kilometers? Let's compare. Canada: kilometers on the road, but people still say their height in feet! Australia: fully metric since the nineteen seventies. Japan: metric, but apartment rooms are often measured in tatami mats. India: switched to metric in the late nineteen fifties. The UK: metric in shops, but road signs are still in miles, and beer comes in pints! But here's the twist. America: uses miles, pounds and Fahrenheit, and it's one of only three countries that never fully went metric, along with Liberia and Myanmar! Miles or kilometers?
