🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇵🇭 Philippines: 7,641 (islands)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: 8,222 (islands)
3. 🇯🇵 Japan: 14,125 (islands)
4. 🇮🇩 Indonesia: 17,000+ (islands)
5. 🇺🇸 USA: ~18,600 (islands)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇸🇪 Sweden: 267,570 (most in the world!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
Which country has the most islands? Let's count up. The Philippines: about seven thousand six hundred islands, and some only appear at low tide. Australia: over eight thousand two hundred, including the famous Fraser Island, now K'gari. Japan: about fourteen thousand, after a new count in twenty twenty three. Indonesia: more than seventeen thousand! America: around eighteen thousand six hundred, counting Alaska and Hawaii. Think that's a lot? Here's the twist. Sweden: about two hundred sixty seven thousand islands, the most in the world, and most have nobody living on them! How many islands does your country have? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
