4. 🇦🇺 Australia: 13% CASH (of payments)
5. 🇬🇧 UK: 12% CASH (of payments)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇸🇪 Sweden: UNDER 10% (some shops refuse cash!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if cash disappeared? Let's see who still uses it. Germany: still loves cash, and many small shops and bakeries don't take cards! Japan: still uses lots of cash too, though phone payments are growing fast. America: most people pay by card now, but some cities require stores to accept cash. Australia: cash is only about thirteen percent of in person payments. The UK: around twelve percent. But here's the twist. Sweden: fewer than one in ten store purchases use cash, some shops won't take it at all, and almost everyone pays with an app called Swish! Cash or card? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
