🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇦🇺 Australia: Some States (QLD vs NSW: 1 hour apart)
2. 🇬🇧 UK: Yes (British Summer Time)
3. 🇯🇵 Japan: No
4. 🇨🇳 China: No (stopped in 1991)
5. 🇷🇺 Russia: Gave Up (tried it 2011-2014)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: STILL CHANGING (Senate voted to stop it in 2022!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if the clocks never changed? Let's check. Australia: only some states change, so in summer, Queensland and New South Wales are an hour apart! The UK: yes, it switches to British Summer Time every March. Japan: no, the clocks never change. China: no, it stopped in nineteen ninety one. Russia: tried permanent summer time in twenty eleven, then gave it up in twenty fourteen! But here's the twist. America: still changes twice a year, except Hawaii and most of Arizona, even though the Senate voted to make summer time permanent back in twenty twenty two! Should clocks still change?
