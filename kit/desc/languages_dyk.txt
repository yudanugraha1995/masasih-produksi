What If Your Country Had 22 Languages? 🗣️
What if your country had more than twenty languages?

💬 How many languages do you speak? Tell us in the comments!

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇦🇺 Australia: None (no official language!)
2. 🇨🇦 Canada: 2 (English + French)
3. 🇨🇭 Switzerland: 4 (incl. Romansh)
4. 🇿🇦 South Africa: 12 (incl. sign language)
5. 🇮🇳 India: 22 (in the constitution!)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: ENGLISH (official only since 2025!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your country had more than twenty languages? Let's count. Australia: no official language at all, even though almost everyone speaks English! Canada: two, English and French, and every product label uses both. Switzerland: four, German, French, Italian and Romansh. South Africa: twelve, including sign language! India: twenty two languages listed in its constitution, and hundreds more spoken! But here's the twist. America: had no official language for almost two hundred fifty years, until English was made official by executive order in twenty twenty five! How many languages do you speak?
