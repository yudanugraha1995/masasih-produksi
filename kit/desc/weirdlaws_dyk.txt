🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your country had a law that sounds made up? Let's look. Singapore: selling chewing gum has been banned since nineteen ninety two! Japan: adults aged forty to seventy four get their waist measured at yearly checkups, by law. Denmark: parents must pick a baby name from a government approved list, or ask for special permission. Germany: running out of fuel on the Autobahn can get you fined, because stopping there is banned. Australia: in Queensland, keeping a pet rabbit is illegal, with fines in the tens of thousands! But here's the twist. America: in more than ten states, car dealers aren't allowed to sell cars on Sundays, thanks to old blue laws! Which law would you change?
