🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your country collected Nobel Prizes? Let's count the winners. Australia: about fifteen, including the scientists who discovered that bacteria cause stomach ulcers! Sweden: about thirty, home of the prize itself, handed out every December. France: about seventy five, including Marie Curie, who won twice! Germany: about one hundred fifteen, including Albert Einstein. The UK: about one hundred forty, including Alexander Fleming, who discovered penicillin. But here's the twist.
