🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇨🇦 Canada: 120 V (flat 2 pin plug)
2. 🇬🇧 UK: 230 V (3 pins + a fuse!)
3. 🇦🇺 Australia: 230 V (slanted pins)
4. 🇨🇭 Switzerland: 230 V (its own plug)
5. 🇯🇵 Japan: 100 V (50 Hz east, 60 Hz west)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: 120 V (slow kettles... use the microwave!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your charger didn't fit the wall? Let's travel with a plug. Canada: one hundred twenty volts, with a flat two pin plug. The UK: two hundred thirty volts, and a chunky three pin plug with a fuse inside! Australia: two hundred thirty volts, with slanted pins. Switzerland: two hundred thirty volts, and its own unique plug. Japan: just one hundred volts, with two different frequencies, fifty in the east and sixty in the west! But here's the twist. America: one hundred twenty volts, so electric kettles boil much slower, which is one reason most Americans heat water in the microwave! What plug do you use? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
