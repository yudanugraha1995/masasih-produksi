🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY

2. 🇪🇸 Spain: 12 GRAPES (one per bell!)
3. 🇯🇵 Japan: 108 BELLS (at temples)
4. 🇧🇷 Brazil: 7 WAVES (wear white + jump!)
5. 🇩🇰 Denmark: Smash Plates (on friends' doors)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: BALL DROP (since 1907... bring diapers!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if New Year's Eve meant smashing plates? Let's look around the world. Australia: huge fireworks over Sydney Harbour, one of the first big cities to welcome the new year. Spain: eat twelve grapes, one for every bell at midnight! Japan: temple bells ring one hundred and eight times. Brazil: wear white and jump over seven waves for luck. Denmark: people smash old plates on their friends' doors for good luck! But here's the twist. America: the Times Square ball has dropped since nineteen oh seven, and so many people pack the streets that some wear adult diapers, because there are no bathrooms! How do you celebrate?
