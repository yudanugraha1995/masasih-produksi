🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: Pancakes (eggs + bacon)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: Vegemite (on toast!)
3. 🇬🇧 UK: Full English (beans + sausages)
4. 🇯🇵 Japan: Rice + Miso (+ grilled fish)
5. 🇲🇽 Mexico: Chilaquiles (chips in salsa)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇳🇱 Netherlands: HAGELSLAG (chocolate sprinkles on bread!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if breakfast was chocolate sprinkles on bread? Let's see. America: pancakes, eggs and bacon, with maple syrup on top. Australia: Vegemite on toast, salty and black! The UK: a full English, with beans, sausages, bacon, eggs and toast. Japan: rice, miso soup and grilled fish, a savory start to the day. Mexico: chilaquiles, tortilla chips cooked in salsa and topped with cheese. But here's the twist. The Netherlands: hagelslag, chocolate sprinkles on buttered bread, and the Dutch eat about seven hundred fifty million slices a year, grown ups too! What did you have for breakfast?
