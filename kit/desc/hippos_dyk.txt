🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if wild hippos lived in your country? Let's check the wild giants around the world. America: Florida has about one point three million alligators! Australia: about one hundred thousand saltwater crocodiles in the Northern Territory alone. India: about three thousand seven hundred wild tigers, three quarters of all wild tigers on Earth. Indonesia: about three thousand Komodo dragons, the biggest lizards alive. South Africa: Kruger Park alone has over three thousand hippos. But here's the twist. Colombia: about two hundred wild hippos, all from just four that drug lord Pablo Escobar brought to his private zoo! Which wild animal would scare you most? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
