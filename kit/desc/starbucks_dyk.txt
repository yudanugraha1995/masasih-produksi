Which Country Has the Most Starbucks? ☕
What if there was a Starbucks on every corner?

💬 Coffee shop or homemade coffee? Tell us in the comments!

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇮🇹 Italy: 2018 (first store, in Milan)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: 100 (stores, most closed in 2008)
3. 🇯🇵 Japan: ~2,000 (incl. a 100 year old house)
4. 🇰🇷 South Korea: ~2,000 (Seoul beats NYC!)
5. 🇨🇳 China: 7,000+ (stores)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: ~17,000 (~40% of all Starbucks!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if there was a Starbucks on every corner? Let's count stores. Italy: Starbucks only arrived in twenty eighteen, because Italians already love their espresso bars. Australia: well under a hundred stores, after closing most of them in two thousand eight! Japan: almost two thousand, including one inside a hundred year old wooden house in Kyoto. South Korea: about two thousand, and Seoul has more Starbucks than New York City. China: over seven thousand, and it keeps growing fast. But here's the twist.
