🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: JULY 4 (1776)
2. 🇲🇽 Mexico: SEPT 16
3. 🇨🇦 Canada: JULY 1 (Canada Day)
4. 🇮🇳 India: AUG 15 (1947)
5. 🇦🇺 Australia: None (Australia Day: Jan 26)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇬🇧 UK: NONE! (60+ countries celebrate independence from it)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your country had no independence day? Let's check. America: July fourth, seventeen seventy six, with fireworks and barbecues. Mexico: September sixteenth, when people shout Viva Mexico the night before. Canada: Canada Day, July first, for confederation in eighteen sixty seven. India: August fifteenth, nineteen forty seven, with kites flying everywhere. Australia: no independence day, Australia Day is January twenty sixth, when the First Fleet arrived. But here's the twist. The UK: no independence day either, and more than sixty countries celebrate independence from it! Does your country have one? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
