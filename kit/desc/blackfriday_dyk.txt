🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY

3. 🇧🇷 Brazil: Black November (a whole month!)
4. 🇲🇽 Mexico: El Buen Fin (since 2011)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: THE ORIGINAL (named by Philadelphia police!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
Is Black Friday the biggest shopping day on Earth? Let's see. The UK: Black Friday too, borrowed from the US just over ten years ago. Australia: now one of its biggest sales of the year. Brazil: Black Friday often stretches into a whole Black November! Mexico: El Buen Fin, its own big sales weekend, started in twenty eleven. China: Singles' Day, on November eleventh, sells even more, mostly online. But here's the twist. America: started it all, and the name came from Philadelphia police in the nineteen sixties, who hated the giant crowds and traffic jams! Do you shop on Black Friday?
