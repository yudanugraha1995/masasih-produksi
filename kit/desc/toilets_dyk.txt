🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if every country had its own toilet rules? Let's take a look. Japan: about eight in ten homes have high tech toilets with a built in bidet, and some even play sounds! Italy: a law from nineteen seventy five says every home needs a bidet. Germany: some older toilets have a little shelf, so you can check before you flush. South Korea: has a toilet theme park, next to a house shaped like a giant toilet! Australia: invented the dual flush toilet in nineteen eighty, saving huge amounts of water. But here's the twist. America: uses about one hundred forty rolls of toilet paper per person a year, among the most on Earth, and public stall doors have big gaps that shock visitors! Bidet or toilet paper?
