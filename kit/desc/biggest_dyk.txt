🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇦🇺 Australia: 7.7M km² (size of mainland US)
2. 🇧🇷 Brazil: 8.5M km²
3. 🇨🇳 China: 9.6M km²
4. 🇺🇸 USA: 9.8M km²
5. 🇨🇦 Canada: 10M km²

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇷🇺 Russia: 17.1M km² (almost 2x Canada!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
Is America the biggest country in the world? Let's measure. Australia: about seven point seven million square kilometers, the size of a whole continent. Brazil: eight and a half million, almost half of South America. China: nine point six million. America: nine point eight million, depending on how you count water. Canada: almost ten million, with more lakes than any other country! Think that's big? Here's the twist. Russia: seventeen point one million square kilometers, almost twice the size of Canada, and it spans eleven time zones! How big is your country? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
