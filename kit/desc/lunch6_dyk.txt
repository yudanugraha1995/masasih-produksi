What If School Lunch Was FREE for Every Kid? 🍱
Did you know Australia has NO national school lunch program? 😮 Finland has fed every student for free since 1948, India feeds over 100 million kids a day, and Indonesia's new free meal program feeds millions!

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇫🇮 Finland: Since 1948 (free for everyone)
2. 🇸🇪 Sweden: Free (for all students)
3. 🇮🇳 India: 100m+ (kids fed every school day)
4. 🇺🇸 USA: ~30m (kids eat school lunch daily)
5. 🇮🇩 Indonesia: Millions (fed by new free meal program)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇦🇺 Australia: NO NATIONAL (school lunch program)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if school lunch was free for every kid? Finland: free for everyone since 1948. Sweden: free for all students. India: over a hundred million kids fed every school day.
