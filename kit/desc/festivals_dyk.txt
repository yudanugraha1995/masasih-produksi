🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your town threw a totally weird festival? Let's look. Spain: La Tomatina, where thousands of people throw over a hundred tons of tomatoes at each other! Thailand: Songkran, a New Year water fight that takes over whole cities. India: Holi, when people cover each other in bright colored powder. The UK: cheese rolling, where people chase a wheel of cheese down a steep hill. Australia: the Melbourne Cup, a horse race so big that Melbourne gets a public holiday! But here's the twist. America: every February, thousands of people wait for a groundhog named Phil to predict the weather, a tradition since eighteen eighty seven! Which festival would you join?
