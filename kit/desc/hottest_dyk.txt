🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇬🇧 UK: 40.3°C (104.5°F, in 2022)
2. 🇪🇸 Spain: 47.6°C (117.7°F, in 2021)
3. 🇨🇦 Canada: 49.6°C (121.3°F, in 2021)
4. 🇦🇺 Australia: 50.7°C (123.3°F)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: 56.7°C (134°F, Death Valley!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
Where is the hottest place on Earth? Let's turn up the heat. The UK: forty point three degrees Celsius, in July twenty twenty two. Spain: forty seven point six, in twenty twenty one. Canada: forty nine point six, in the town of Lytton, which burned down the next day! Australia: fifty point seven, in Oodnadatta. Kuwait: fifty three point nine, one of the hottest readings in Asia! Think that's hot? Here's the twist. America: Death Valley hit fifty six point seven degrees in nineteen thirteen, that's one hundred thirty four Fahrenheit, the hottest ever recorded on Earth!
