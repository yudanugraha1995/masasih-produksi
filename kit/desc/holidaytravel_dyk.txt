What if holiday traffic was nine billion trips? Let's compare the biggest trips home. Australia: Christmas is summer break, so airports and highways are packed for weeks. The UK: the days before Christmas bring millions of car journeys. America: about eighty million people travel for Thanksgiving! Indonesia: about one hundred ninety million people go home for Mudik, at the end of Ramadan. India: the Kumbh Mela festival counted over six hundred million visits in twenty twenty five!
