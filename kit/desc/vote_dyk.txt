You Get FINED for Not Voting Here 🗳️
Did you know Australians get FINED if they don't vote? 🗳️😤 Voting there has been compulsory since 1924. Belgium and Brazil make it mandatory too, Singapore can remove you from the voter roll, while in the USA and UK voting is totally voluntary.

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇧🇪 Belgium: Mandatory (since 1893)
2. 🇧🇷 Brazil: Mandatory (small fine)
3. 🇸🇬 Singapore: Removed (from the voter roll)
4. 🇺🇸 USA: Voluntary (millions skip it)
5. 🇬🇧 UK: Voluntary

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇦🇺 Australia: $20 FINE (compulsory since 1924)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
Can you get fined for not voting? Belgium: yes, voting has been mandatory since 1893. Brazil: mandatory, with a small fine. Singapore: skip it and you're removed from the voter roll. America: voluntary, and millions skip it. The UK: voluntary too. Plot twist: Australia has made everyone vote since 1924. Skip it and you pay a twenty dollar fine! Comment your country!
