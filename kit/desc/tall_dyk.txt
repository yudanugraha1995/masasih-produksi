🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇳🇱 Netherlands: 184 cm (men, tallest on Earth)
2. 🇰🇷 South Korea: +20 cm (women in 100 years!)
3. 🇮🇷 Iran: +16 cm (men, biggest jump)
4. 🇯🇵 Japan: 171 cm (men)
5. 🇦🇺 Australia: 179 cm (men)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: #37 (from #3 a century ago!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your country decided how tall you grow? Let's compare. The Netherlands: men average about one hundred eighty four centimeters, the tallest on Earth! South Korea: women grew about twenty centimeters in a century, the biggest jump ever recorded. Iran: men grew about sixteen centimeters, the biggest jump for men. Japan: men average about one seventy one, but that's way taller than a hundred years ago. Australia: men average about one seventy nine, taller than most Brits. But here's the twist. America: a century ago, American men were the third tallest in the world, but now they've slipped to around thirty seventh, with an average of about one seventy seven! Are you taller than average?
