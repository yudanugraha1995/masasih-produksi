Which Country Has the Weirdest Schools? 🏫
What if your school had a strange rule?

💬 What was the weirdest rule at your school? Tell us in the comments!

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇯🇵 Japan: Cleaning (by students!)
2. 🇫🇮 Finland: AGE 7 (little homework)
3. 🇰🇷 South Korea: 10 PM (cram school curfew)
4. 🇫🇷 France: Wednesday (afternoons off)
5. 🇦🇺 Australia: Radio (School of the Air)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: ~480,000 (yellow school buses!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your school had a strange rule? Let's look. Japan: students clean their own classrooms and hallways every day, no janitors! Finland: kids start school at seven, and get very little homework. South Korea: many teens study at cram schools so late that Seoul set a ten p m curfew. France: many primary schools have no classes on Wednesday afternoons. Australia: outback kids join the School of the Air, learning from home over radio and now the internet! But here's the twist. America: about half a million yellow school buses, the biggest mass transit fleet in the whole country! What was the weirdest rule at your school? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
