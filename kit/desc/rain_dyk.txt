🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if it rained almost every day where you live? After record rain in California, let's compare yearly rainfall. Australia: about five hundred thirty millimeters, the driest inhabited continent. America: about seven hundred fifteen millimeters on average. The UK: about one thousand two hundred, famous for its drizzle! Japan: about one thousand seven hundred, with a whole rainy season in June. Colombia: about three thousand two hundred, one of the wettest countries on Earth! But here's the twist. India: the village of Mawsynram gets about eleven thousand eight hundred millimeters a year, the rainiest place on Earth! Do you love the rain? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
