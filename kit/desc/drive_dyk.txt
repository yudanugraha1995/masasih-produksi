How Young Can You Drive ALONE? 🚗
Did you know teens in South Dakota, USA can drive alone at just 14 and a half? 🚗😱 Canada 16, UK 17, Australia 17, Germany and Japan 18… the minimum age to drive alone changes a LOT around the world.

💬 How old do you have to be to drive in your country? Tell us in the comments 👇

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇨🇦 Canada: 16 (in most provinces)
2. 🇬🇧 UK: 17 (full licence)
3. 🇦🇺 Australia: 17 (in most states)
4. 🇩🇪 Germany: 18 (17 with an adult)
5. 🇯🇵 Japan: 18

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 South Dakota, USA: 14½ (drive alone (restricted permit))

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
How young can you drive alone? Canada: sixteen in most provinces. The UK: seventeen. Australia: seventeen in most states. Germany: eighteen, or seventeen with an adult riding along. Japan: eighteen. Plot twist: in South Dakota, USA, teens can drive alone at just fourteen and a half! Would you let a fourteen-year-old drive you? Comment your country!
