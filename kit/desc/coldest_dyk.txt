How cold can it get where people live?

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇦🇺 Australia: -23°C (-9.4°F)
2. 🇬🇧 UK: -27.2°C (-17°F)
3. 🇳🇴 Norway: -51.4°C (-60.5°F)
4. 🇺🇸 USA: -62.2°C (-80°F, Alaska)
5. 🇨🇦 Canada: -63°C (-81.4°F, Yukon)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇷🇺 Russia: -67.8°C (and people live there!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
How cold can it get where people live? Let's find out. Australia: minus twenty three degrees Celsius, in the Snowy Mountains. The UK: minus twenty seven point two, in Scotland. Norway: minus fifty one point four! America: minus sixty two point two, in Alaska. Canada: minus sixty three, in the Yukon, so cold that people's breath made a hissing sound! Think that's cold? Here's the twist. Russia: minus sixty seven point eight degrees in Oymyakon, and about five hundred people still live there all year, where kids only stay home from school when it drops below about minus fifty!
