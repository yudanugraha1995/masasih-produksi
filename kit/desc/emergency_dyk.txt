What If 911 Didn't Work? 🚨
What if nine one one didn't work where you live?

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇦🇺 Australia: 000 (triple zero)
2. 🇬🇧 UK: 999 (oldest, since 1937!)
3. 🇳🇿 New Zealand: 111
4. 🇯🇵 Japan: 119 / 110 (ambulance / police)
5. 🇩🇪 Germany: 112 (works across the EU)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: 911 (only since 1968!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if nine one one didn't work where you live? Here's who to call. Australia: triple zero. The UK: nine nine nine, the oldest emergency number in the world, since nineteen thirty seven! New Zealand: one one one, which on old New Zealand phones dialed just like Britain's nine nine nine! Japan: one one nine for fire and ambulance, and one one zero for police. Germany: one one two, which works all across the European Union. But here's the twist. America: nine one one only started in nineteen sixty eight, in a small town in Alabama, and today it handles about two hundred forty million calls a year! What's your emergency number?
