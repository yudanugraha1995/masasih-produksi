What If You Couldn't Retire Until 70? 👴
Did you know Denmark voted to raise its retirement age to 70, the highest planned pension age in the world? Here's how it compares with Japan, the UK, the USA and Australia.

💬 When can you retire in your country? Tell us in the comments!

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇯🇵 Japan: 65 (pension age)
2. 🇬🇧 UK: 66 (State Pension age)
3. 🇺🇸 USA: 67 (full Social Security)
4. 🇦🇺 Australia: 67 (Age Pension)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇩🇰 Denmark: 70 (by 2040!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if you couldn't retire until seventy? In Japan, the pension age is sixty-five. The UK, sixty-six. America and Australia, sixty-seven. Think that's late? Denmark just voted to push it to seventy by 2040! When can you retire in your country? Comment below, and subscribe!
