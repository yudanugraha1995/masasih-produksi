🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇳🇿 New Zealand: 85 (letter hill name!)
2. 🇹🇭 Thailand: 160+ (letters for Bangkok)
3. 🇬🇧 UK: 58 (letter Welsh village)
4. 🇩🇪 Germany: 63 (letter law name)
5. 🇦🇺 Australia: Nowhere Else (is a real town!)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: 45 (letter lake name!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your hometown had a name nobody could say? Let's look. New Zealand: a hill with an eighty five letter Māori name, one of the longest place names on Earth! Thailand: the full ceremonial name of Bangkok has over one hundred sixty letters. The UK: a Welsh village with a fifty eight letter name, and its train station sign is a tourist attraction. Germany: a sixty three letter word for a beef labeling law was officially scrapped in twenty thirteen. Australia: has real towns called Nowhere Else and Come By Chance! But here's the twist. America: a lake in Massachusetts has a forty five letter name, and even locals struggle to spell it! Can you say your town's name fast? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
