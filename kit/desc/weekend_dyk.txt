🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇦🇪 UAE: SAT + SUN (since 2022, half day Friday)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your weekend was Friday and Saturday? Let's compare. America: Saturday and Sunday, a two day weekend that spread after Henry Ford gave factory workers two days off in nineteen twenty six. Australia: Saturday and Sunday too, often spent at the beach or watching sport. Saudi Arabia: Friday and Saturday, since twenty thirteen. Egypt: Friday and Saturday too, with Friday prayers at the heart of the week. Kuwait: also Friday and Saturday. But here's the twist. The UAE: it switched to a Saturday and Sunday weekend in twenty twenty two, with a half day on Friday, to match the rest of the world's markets! Which days should be the weekend?
