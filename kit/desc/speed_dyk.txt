What If Your Country Had NO Speed Limit? 🏎️
Did you know many stretches of Germany's autobahn have NO speed limit at all? 🏎️🤯 The UK caps motorways at 70 mph, Japan and parts of Canada at 120 km/h, Australia's Northern Territory goes up to 130 km/h and one Texas toll road allows 85 mph.

💬 What's the fastest speed limit in your country? Tell us in the comments 👇

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇬🇧 UK: 70 mph (on motorways)
2. 🇯🇵 Japan: 120 km/h (on some expressways)
3. 🇨🇦 Canada: 120 km/h (in some provinces)
4. 🇦🇺 Australia: 130 km/h (Northern Territory)
5. 🇺🇸 USA: 85 mph (Texas toll road)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇩🇪 Germany: NO LIMIT (on many autobahn stretches!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your country had no speed limit? The UK: seventy miles per hour on motorways. Japan: one hundred twenty kilometers per hour. Canada: up to one hundred twenty. Australia: one hundred thirty, in the Northern Territory. America: up to eighty-five miles per hour, in Texas. Plot twist: Germany. On the autobahn, many stretches have no speed limit at all!
