🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your country controlled the music charts? Let's look. Japan: still buys more CDs than almost anywhere, and it's the world's second biggest music market. Sweden: Spotify started in Stockholm, and Swedish producer Max Martin has written over twenty five American number one hits! South Korea: BTS became the first South Korean act to top the Billboard Hot 100. The UK: the Beatles still hold the record for the most Hot 100 number ones, twenty! Australia: AC/DC's Back in Black is one of the best selling albums of all time. But here's the twist. America: the biggest music market on Earth, bringing in roughly a third of all the world's recorded music money! Which artist do you play the most? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
