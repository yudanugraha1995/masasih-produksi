🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your biggest city wasn't the capital? It happens a lot. America: the capital is Washington DC, not New York, which has more than ten times as many people. Australia: Canberra, not Sydney, built as a compromise between Sydney and Melbourne! Canada: Ottawa, not Toronto, picked by Queen Victoria. Brazil: Brasília, not São Paulo, a whole new city built in about four years. Turkey: Ankara, not Istanbul, even though Istanbul is far more famous. But here's the twist. South Africa: it has three capitals, Pretoria for the government, Cape Town for parliament, and Bloemfontein for the top appeals court! Did you know your capital?
