🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your country still had a king or queen? Let's look. Japan: has the oldest hereditary monarchy in the world, and its emperor has no political power. Denmark: one of the oldest kingdoms in Europe, and its queen gave up the throne in twenty twenty four. Saudi Arabia: one of the last countries where the king holds almost total power. The UK: King Charles is also the king of fourteen other countries! Australia: voted to keep the king as head of state in a nineteen ninety nine referendum. But here's the twist.
