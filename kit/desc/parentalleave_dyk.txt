🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇦🇺 Australia: 26 WEEKS (government paid)
2. 🇬🇧 UK: 39 WEEKS (statutory pay)
3. 🇨🇦 Canada: 18 MONTHS (of benefits)
4. 🇳🇴 Norway: 49 WEEKS (at full pay)
5. 🇸🇪 Sweden: 480 DAYS (shared by both parents!)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: 0 WEEKS PAID (12 weeks unpaid (FMLA))

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if new parents got more than a year off? Let's compare. Australia: twenty six weeks of government paid leave. The UK: up to thirty nine weeks of statutory pay. Canada: up to eighteen months of benefits, if parents choose the extended option. Norway: forty nine weeks at full pay, with fifteen weeks just for dad. Sweden: four hundred eighty days, shared by both parents! But here's the twist. America: no national paid parental leave at all, just twelve weeks unpaid for some workers, making it one of the only rich countries without it! How long should parental leave be?
