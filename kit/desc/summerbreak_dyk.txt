What If Summer Break Was 3 Months? ☀️
What if summer break lasted three months?

💬 How long is your summer break? Tell us in the comments!

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇦🇺 Australia: ~6 WEEKS (over Christmas!)
2. 🇬🇧 UK: ~6 WEEKS (late July to September)
3. 🇯🇵 Japan: ~6 WEEKS (from late July)
4. 🇩🇪 Germany: 6 WEEKS (dates vary by state)
5. 🇺🇸 USA: 10-11 WEEKS

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇮🇹 Italy: ~13 WEEKS (mid June to mid September!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if summer break lasted three months? Let's compare. Australia: about six weeks, over Christmas, because it's summer there! The UK: about six weeks, from late July to early September. Japan: about six weeks, from late July, but kids still get a lot of homework. Germany: six weeks, and each state starts at a different time so the highways don't jam. America: around ten to eleven weeks, depending on the state! Think that's long? Here's the twist. Italy: about thirteen weeks, from mid June to mid September, one of the longest summer breaks in Europe! How long is your summer break? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
