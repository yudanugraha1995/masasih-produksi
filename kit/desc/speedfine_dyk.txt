🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: Flat Fine (same for everyone)
2. 🇦🇺 Australia: Flat Fine (same for everyone)
3. 🇬🇧 UK: Income-Based (set by weekly income)
4. 🇩🇪 Germany: Day Fines (for serious offenses)
5. 🇨🇭 Switzerland: $100,000+ (record fines)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇫🇮 Finland: €121,000 (one speeding ticket!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if your speeding ticket depended on how much you earn? America: usually a flat fine, the same for everyone, set by each state. Australia: a flat fine too, plus demerit points on your license. The UK: courts can set fines based on your weekly income. Germany: day fines, based on income, for serious offenses. Switzerland: income based, and one driver got a fine of over one hundred thousand dollars! But here's the twist. Finland: one millionaire got a speeding ticket of one hundred twenty one thousand euros, for going thirty kilometers per hour over the limit! Should fines depend on income?
