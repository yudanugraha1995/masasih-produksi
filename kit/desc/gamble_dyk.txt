🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if you weren't allowed into a casino in your own country? America: you usually need to be twenty one, and Las Vegas has more than forty big casinos. Australia: eighteen, and Australians lose more per person on gambling than anyone else. The UK: eighteen too. Japan: no casinos yet, the first one is planned in Osaka. Singapore: locals pay a one hundred fifty dollar entry fee every day, while tourists get in free! But here's the twist. South Korea: locals are banned from every casino except one, called Kangwon Land, up in the mountains! Would you go to a casino? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
