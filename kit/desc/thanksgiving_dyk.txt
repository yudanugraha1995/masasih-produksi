What If Thanksgiving Was in October? 🦃
What if Thanksgiving happened on a totally different day?

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇨🇦 Canada: OCT 12 (2nd Monday of October)
2. 🇩🇪 Germany: Early Oct (Erntedankfest)
3. 🇰🇷 South Korea: Chuseok (3 day harvest holiday)
4. 🇯🇵 Japan: NOV 23 (Labor Thanksgiving Day)
5. 🇦🇺 Australia: Norfolk Island (American style Thanksgiving!)

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇺🇸 USA: 46 MILLION (turkeys in one day!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if Thanksgiving happened on a totally different day? Let's check around the world. Canada: the second Monday of October, more than a month earlier! Germany: a harvest festival called Erntedankfest, in early October. South Korea: Chuseok, a three day harvest holiday when families travel home. Japan: Labor Thanksgiving Day, every November twenty third, to thank workers. Australia: on tiny Norfolk Island, people celebrate an American style Thanksgiving, thanks to visiting whalers! But here's the twist. America: the fourth Thursday of November, when Americans eat about forty six million turkeys in a single day! When do you give thanks? Comment below, and subscribe for more!
