What If You Had to Wait Until 21 to Drink? 🍺
Did you know 16-year-olds in Germany can legally buy beer and wine? 🍺😳 In the USA you have to wait until 21, in Japan until 20, in Canada 18 or 19 depending on the province, and in the UK and Australia 18.

💬 What's the legal drinking age in your country? Tell us in the comments 👇

🌍 COUNTRY BY COUNTRY
1. 🇺🇸 USA: 21 (strictest in the rich world)
2. 🇯🇵 Japan: 20 (even though adults are 18)
3. 🇨🇦 Canada: 18-19 (depends on the province)
4. 🇬🇧 UK: 18
5. 🇦🇺 Australia: 18

🤯 PLOT TWIST: 🇩🇪 Germany: 16 (for beer and wine!)

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if you had to wait until twenty-one to drink? America: twenty-one, the strictest in the rich world. Japan: twenty, even though adults are eighteen.
